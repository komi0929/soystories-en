Gluten-Free Sweets & Café in Fukuoka

SoyStories is a 100% wheat-free (gluten-free) and 100% plant-based (dairy-free and egg-free) sweets café in Yakuin, Fukuoka. Visit daily from 11:00 to 22:00. Yakuin-odori is about 4 minutes by subway from Tenjin-minami or 9 minutes from Hakata, plus a short walk.

Dedicated wheat-free kitchen

Wheat, dairy, eggs, honey, gelatin, and refined white sugar are never brought into our kitchen.

Our shared kitchen handles soybeans, almonds, cashews, pistachios, macadamia nuts, sesame, Japanese yam, apples, peaches, and oranges. Trace cross-contact is possible.

Check the 29-allergen menu table and FoodHug ingredient disclosure. If you have a severe allergy, check with staff before ordering.

Getting here from Hakata, Tenjin or Fukuoka Airport

SoyStories is open daily from 11:00 to 22:00, with last orders at 21:45.

Take the Nanakuma Line to Yakuin-odori Station and walk 2–3 minutes from Exit 2. The subway ride takes about 9 minutes from Hakata or 4 minutes from Tenjin-minami. From Fukuoka Airport, take the Kuko (Airport) Line to Hakata and transfer to the Nanakuma Line. Allow about 20–25 minutes by subway, plus the walk.

Find us on the first floor of Chisan Mansion Dai-3 Hakata, 2-2-24 Yakuin, Chuo-ku, Fukuoka 810-0022.

We accept yen cash, major cards, Suica and other Japanese transit IC cards, and PayPay. Suitcases and strollers are welcome.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find gluten-free dessert near Hakata or Tenjin?

SoyStories is a 100% wheat-free and plant-based sweets café in Yakuin, Fukuoka. Yakuin-odori is about 9 minutes by subway from Hakata or 4 minutes from Tenjin-minami, followed by a 2–3 minute walk from Exit 2.

Is the kitchen dedicated wheat-free?

Wheat, dairy, eggs, honey, gelatin, and refined white sugar are never brought into our kitchen. Our shared kitchen handles soybeans, almonds, cashews, pistachios, macadamia nuts, sesame, Japanese yam, apples, peaches, and oranges. Trace cross-contact is possible.

Can I get a gluten-free waffle in Fukuoka?

SoyStories bakes rice-flour bubble waffles to order, from ¥660. Waffle Soft with pure soy milk soft serve is ¥690 for medium or ¥900 for large.

What time does the gluten-free café close?

SoyStories is open daily from 11:00 to 22:00, with last orders at 21:45.
