Ingredients & Dietary Policy

Check what’s right for your diet

Wheat, Dairy & Egg-Free Kitchen Policy

Every item at SoyStories is 100% plant-based (vegan) and gluten-free. Wheat, dairy, eggs, and animal-derived ingredients (including honey and gelatin) are never brought into our kitchen (including staff personal meals for wheat). We also use no refined white sugar (sweetened gently with beet sugar and plant sweeteners).

  • No wheat / gluten
  • No dairy
  • No eggs
  • No honey
  • No gelatin
  • No white sugar
Important Notice for Guests with Severe Allergies or Celiac Disease:
Within our kitchen, we handle soybeans, almonds, sesame, Japanese yam (yamaimo), apples, peaches, and oranges (and certain delivery/seasonal items such as vegan tiramisu may use cashew nuts, a mandatory specified allergen in Japan as of April 2026). While we take the utmost care with hygiene and equipment, complete elimination of trace cross-contact cannot be guaranteed. Guests with severe allergies or celiac disease are strongly encouraged to check the per-item list below and consult our staff in advance or at the counter.

Our Story & Craft

A “Yumemitai” (Dream-Like) Table for Everyone

Sharing the same delicious moment together

Our mission at SoyStories is to create a “Yumemitai” (dream-like) place for everyone — whether you follow a vegan lifestyle, live with food allergies, or simply love pure Japanese ingredients.

The happiness of sitting at the same table with family and friends and enjoying the exact same sweet treats without worry — to bring that joy to life, we bake and swirl our sweets fresh in Yakuin every day.

  • Fresh Morning Soy Milk (Processed Within 48 Hours)Additive-free concentrated soy milk from local Fukuoka tofu craftsmen, processed within 48 hours of pressing with delicate temperature control for a rich, clean finish.
  • 100% Domestic Niigata Rice FlourFinely milled Japanese rice flour gives our bubble waffles a golden, crispy exterior and a warm, mochi-chewy interior without a gram of wheat.
  • Okara (Soy Pulp), Japanese Yam & Beet SugarRich in natural fiber and plant protein, sweetened gently with beet sugar and natural plant sweeteners — zero refined white sugar.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting

1. Are all products 100% vegan (plant-based)?

Yes. Every single sweet and drink at SoyStories Yakuin is 100% plant-based (vegan). We never use meat, fish, dairy, eggs, honey, or gelatin in any product.

2. What is your gluten-free policy? Can guests with celiac disease or wheat allergies eat here?

All of our products are made with 100% gluten-free rice flour or almond flour, and wheat, dairy, and eggs are never brought into our kitchen (including staff personal food for wheat). However, because we handle soybeans, almonds, sesame, Japanese yam, apples, peaches, and oranges in the same kitchen (and certain seasonal/delivery items such as vegan tiramisu may use cashew nuts), complete elimination of trace allergens cannot be guaranteed. Guests with severe allergies or celiac disease are encouraged to review per-item ingredients and consult our staff before ordering.

3. How can I check per-item ingredients and allergens?

You can use the interactive Allergen Checker and the Complete 26-Item Menu Table on this page, check the allergen badges on each menu category page, or view our Official FoodHug Allergy Disclosure Page. You can also ask our counter staff directly.

4. Do your products contain soy, tree nuts (almonds/cashews), sesame, or Japanese yam?

It depends on the product:
• Soybeans: Used in Bubble Waffles, Soy Soft Serve, Soy Whip, Baked Soy Donuts, and 4 Gelato flavors. Soy-free options include all 6 Glazed Cake Donuts (¥430) and 4 Fruit Gelato flavors (Berry Mix, Juicy Dragon Fruit, Refreshing Apple, Too Juicy Peach).
• Almonds (Tree Nuts): Used in Glazed Cake Donuts (¥430), Melting Chocolate Gelato, and the optional Okara Cookie topping. (Note: Certain seasonal/delivery items like vegan tiramisu may use cashew paste.)
• Sesame: Used in the Black Sesame Bubble Waffle and Black Sesame Soy Donut.
• Japanese Yam (Yamaimo): Used in the 6 Baked Soy Donuts (¥330) for chewiness.

5. Do you use white sugar? Are your sweets “sugar-free”?

We use no refined white sugar. Instead, our sweets are gently sweetened with beet sugar, organic agave/maple, or natural plant sweeteners depending on the item. Please note that this means they are free from refined white sugar, not zero-sugar / sugar-free.

6. What are your opening hours, last order time, and holidays?

We are open daily from 11:00 AM to 10:00 PM (22:00), with Last Order at 9:45 PM (21:45) and no regular holidays. Any temporary hour changes or special holidays are announced on Instagram (@soystories_yakuin).

7. Can items sell out? Can I pre-order or hold items?

Bubble waffles and soy soft serve are prepared fresh until last order (9:45 PM), though specific donut or gelato flavors can sell out later in the evening. To reserve specific flavors or gift boxes, send us a DM on Instagram (@soystories_yakuin).

8. How many dine-in seats do you have? Can I bring a stroller or suitcase?

We have a compact dine-in space with 4 main seats plus wooden cube stools. Strollers and suitcases are welcome inside, there is no minimum order per person, and sharing items is welcome.

9. Which items are best for takeaway, and how long can I carry them?

• Bubble Waffles & Soy Soft Serve: Available for takeaway, but because warm waffles melt soft serve quickly, we strongly recommend eating them immediately in-store or right outside.
• Glazed Cake Donuts (¥430): Keep for about 1 week at room temperature — our best choice for hotel snacks, bullet trains, and gifts.
• Baked Soy Donuts (¥330): Sold frozen (or warmed in-store). If carried at room temperature, they thaw naturally; keep refrigerated and enjoy within 3 days.
• Cup Craft Gelato (¥490): Sold frozen; cooler bags with ice packs are available (free when buying 4 or more items).

10. How do I eat or reheat the frozen Baked Soy Donuts?

If you want to eat a Baked Soy Donut right away, ask our staff to warm it up in-store! If taking it back to your hotel or home, heat it in a 600W microwave for about 1 minute (or toast lightly after thawing) for a freshly baked mochi-chewy texture.

11. What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash (JPY), major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, JCB, American Express, Diners Club), Japanese transit IC cards (Suica, PASMO, ICOCA, SUGOCA, nimoca, Hayakaken), iD, QUICPay, and PayPay. All menu prices include tax.

12. How do I get there from Yakuin-odori, Tenjin, or Hakata Station?

Take the Fukuoka City Subway Nanakuma Line to Yakuin-odori Station (薬院大通駅), take Exit 2, and walk 2–3 minutes (~160m) toward Yakuin Mutsukado. It is only ~4 minutes by subway from Tenjin-minami Station and ~9 minutes from Hakata Station. Look for our 1st-floor storefront with the glowing soft-serve sign.

13. Is there an English/Korean/Chinese menu? What if I don't speak Japanese?

We have printed English, Korean, and Traditional Chinese menus at the counter. Even if staff on shift speak basic English, you can tap the “Show staff” button on any item on this website to display a clear Japanese ordering card (with Eat-In / Takeout toggle) on your phone screen.
