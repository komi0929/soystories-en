FAQ

Good to know

Is it safe for celiac disease?

Our kitchen is 100% dedicated: wheat, dairy, eggs and other animal-derived ingredients are never brought in. We also use no honey or gelatin. For other allergens, use the allergen checker above or see our allergy policy.

How many seats? Can I bring a stroller or suitcase?

We have 12 indoor seats on wooden cube stools. Strollers and suitcases are welcome, there’s no minimum order, and sharing is fine. One seat has a power outlet.

What time is last order?

We’re open daily 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM. Last order is 9:45 PM.

Can I take sweets back to my hotel or home as souvenirs?

Yes, every item can be taken out. Boxed glazed cake donuts keep about 1 week at room temperature. Soy donuts are sold frozen — we can warm one for you in-store, or microwave at 600W for about 1 minute (or thaw naturally, keep refrigerated and enjoy within 3 days). For ice cream we have cooler bags, free with an ice pack when you buy 4 or more.

Can I reserve or pre-order?

You can pre-order / hold donuts and other items. Sending us a DM on Instagram (@soystories_yakuin) is the most reliable way.

Payment methods? English menu?

We have an English menu at the counter. We accept cash (JPY), credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, JCB, American Express, Diners Club), transit IC cards (Suica, PASMO, ICOCA, SUGOCA, nimoca, Hayakaken, etc.), iD, QUICPay and PayPay. All prices include tax.

Is there parking?

We don’t have our own parking lot, but there are many coin-operated parking lots right next to the shop.
