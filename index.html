Concept

A “Yumemitai” (Dream-Come-True) Dessert Experience for Everyone

Our mission at SoyStories is to create a “Yumemitai” (dream-come-true) place for everyone—whether you live with food allergies or celiac disease, follow a vegan lifestyle, or simply care deeply about pure, natural ingredients.

The happiness of sitting around the same table and sharing the exact same delicious treats together with family and friends...
To bring that joy to life, we pour our heart into our shop every single day.

Dairy-Free (No Milk) Egg-Free (No Eggs) Gluten-Free (No Wheat)

Every single item at SoyStories is 100% Gluten-Free & 100% Plant-Based (Vegan).
We never use wheat, dairy, eggs, or refined white sugar in any of our products.
Whether you have dietary restrictions or not, we promise a rich, unforgettable dessert experience with complete peace of mind.

Celiac & Allergy Safe

Our Strict Allergy & Zero Cross-Contamination Commitment

1. Dedicated Wheat, Dairy & Egg-Free Kitchen

Wheat, milk (dairy), and eggs are never brought into our kitchen or manufacturing facility. You can enjoy our waffles, soft serve, donuts, and gelato without worrying about shared equipment or airborne wheat flour.

2. Soy-Free & 28-Allergen-Free Options Available

Can’t eat soy? Our Moist Cake Donuts (6 flavors, almond & rice flour base) are 100% soy-free! Plus, several of our Rice Flour Craft Gelato flavors (Berry Mix, Juicy Dragon Fruit, Refreshing Apple, Too Juicy Peach) are soy-free and nut-free.

3. English Menu & Transparent Allergen Icons

Every menu item clearly lists all specific allergens used (such as Soybeans, Almonds, Black Sesame, Japanese Yam, or Apple). We also provide an English menu at the counter and follow a strict staff allergy manual.

Our Craft & Ingredients

Why Our Dairy-Free & Gluten-Free Sweets Taste So Rich

The ultra-rich soy milk we use is 100% additive-free.
To lock in peak freshness and natural sweetness with zero grassy aftertaste, we complete all processing within 48 hours of pressing the soybeans.
Temperature control for fresh soy milk is far more delicate than for dairy milk—requiring artisanal precision to draw out only the pure umami and creaminess of Japanese soybeans.

INGREDIENT 01

Okara (Soy Pulp)

Packed with dietary fiber & high-quality plant protein for a moist, satisfying texture.

INGREDIENT 02

Niigata Rice Flour

100% domestic rice flour from Niigata, Japan—giving our waffles a crispy outside & mochi-chewy inside.

INGREDIENT 03

Morning Soy Milk

Freshly squeezed morning soy milk with extraordinary richness—processed within 48 hours.

01

Proprietary Rice Flour Creaminess

Plant-based ice creams often feel icy or thin. By harnessing the natural starch structure of premium Japanese rice flour, we achieve a slow-melting, Italian-gelato-level smoothness without a single drop of dairy cream or stabilizer chemicals.

02

Japanese Fermentation & Natural Sweeteners

Instead of refined white sugar, we use natural beet sugar (Tensai-to) and traditional Japanese fermented ingredients like sweet rice Koji (Amazake) and a touch of wheat-free white miso to create deep, lingering caramel-like richness.

03

Dedicated Allergen-Free Workshop

Every donut, waffle, and cup of gelato is handcrafted in our dedicated facility where wheat, dairy, and eggs are strictly prohibited—so guests with severe allergies or celiac disease never have to second-guess a single bite.

FAQ

Visitor Questions

Q01

Do you have indoor seating for Eat-In, or is it Takeout only?

We have 4 cozy indoor seats where you are welcome to relax and dine in! Every item on our menu is also available for takeout.
For eat-in, we highly recommend our freshly baked Waffle & Soy Soft Serve. For takeout or hotel snacks, our Baked Donuts and Rice Flour Craft Gelato are customer favorites.

Q02

How do I eat the takeout donuts? Can I eat them right away at the shop?

To preserve their freshly baked moisture without preservatives, our donuts are sold flash-frozen.
Want to eat your donut right away? Just let our staff know—we can warm it up for you in the shop so you can enjoy it warm, soft, and cake-like on the spot!
If taking them back to your hotel, simply microwave at 600W for about 1 minute (or let thaw naturally; keep refrigerated after thawing and enjoy within 3 days).

600W / 1 minute (or warmed in-store!)

Q03

How do I get there by subway? Is there parking?

By Subway: We are just a 3-minute walk from Yakuin-odori Station (Exit 2) on the Fukuoka City Subway Nanakuma Line—only ~9 minutes directly from Hakata Station and ~4 minutes from Tenjin-minami Station!
By Car: We do not have a dedicated shop parking lot, so please use one of the many coin-operated parking lots right next to the shop.

Q04

Is SoyStories safe for Celiac Disease and severe Dairy, Egg, or Wheat allergies?

Yes! All products at our shop are 100% free from wheat (gluten), dairy (milk), eggs, and refined white sugar, and we never bring those ingredients into our kitchen.
If you also have a soy allergy, our 6 Moist Cake Donuts (almond & rice flour base) and 4 fruit Rice Flour Craft Gelato flavors are completely soy-free. For full details on our kitchen protocols, please visit our official English Allergy Policy page:
→ View Official English Allergy Policy (food-hug.com)

Q05

What payment methods do you accept? Do you have an English menu?

Yes, we have an English menu at the counter!
In addition to Japanese Yen (Cash), we accept:
• Major Credit Cards: Visa, Mastercard, JCB, American Express, Diners Club
• Transit IC Cards: Suica, PASMO, ICOCA, SUGOCA, nimoca, Hayakaken, etc.
• E-Money & QR: iD, QUICPay, and PayPay.
