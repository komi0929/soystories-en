Celiac-Safe & Transparent Allergen Guide (Zero Wheat, Dairy, Eggs, Honey or Gelatin)

1. 100% Dedicated Kitchen (Celiac-Safe)

Our kitchen is a 100% dedicated gluten-free and plant-based space. Wheat, barley, rye, milk (dairy), eggs, meat, fish, honey, and gelatin are never brought into our kitchen or manufacturing facility—zero risk of shared equipment or airborne wheat flour.

2. Nut-Free & Soy-Free Choices Clearly Separated

• Nut-Free: Our Rice Flour Bubble Waffles, Pure Soy Soft Serve, and Plain Baked Soy Donuts (¥330) contain NO nuts.
• Almond Note: Only our Glazed Moist Cake Donuts (¥430), Melting Chocolate gelato, and the optional Okara Cookie topping contain Almonds.
• Soy-Free: All 6 Glazed Moist Cake Donuts (¥430) and 4 fruit Craft Gelatos are 100% Soy-Free!

3. Black Sesame & Other Specific Allergens

• Sesame: Used only in our Black Sesame Soy Donut and Black Sesame Bubble Waffle (all other flavors are 100% sesame-free).
• Japanese Yam (Yamaimo): Used in our chewy Plain Baked Soy Donuts.
• Apple / Peach: Used in select fruit gelatos and glazed cake donuts.

Our Craft & Ingredients

Why Our Dairy-Free & Gluten-Free Sweets Taste So Rich

The ultra-rich soy milk we use is 100% additive-free.
To lock in peak freshness and natural sweetness with zero grassy aftertaste, we complete all processing within 48 hours of pressing the soybeans.
Temperature control for fresh soy milk is far more delicate than for dairy milk—requiring artisanal precision to draw out only the pure umami and creaminess of Japanese soybeans.

INGREDIENT 01

Okara (Soy Pulp)

Packed with dietary fiber & high-quality plant protein for a moist, satisfying texture.

INGREDIENT 02

Niigata Rice Flour

100% domestic rice flour from Niigata, Japan—giving our waffles a crispy outside & mochi-chewy inside.

INGREDIENT 03

Morning Soy Milk

Freshly squeezed morning soy milk with extraordinary richness—processed within 48 hours.

01

Proprietary Rice Flour Creaminess

Plant-based ice creams often feel icy or thin. By harnessing the natural starch structure of premium Japanese rice flour, we achieve a slow-melting, Italian-gelato-level smoothness without a single drop of dairy cream or stabilizer chemicals.

02

Japanese Fermentation & Natural Sweeteners

Instead of refined white sugar, we use natural beet sugar (Tensai-to) and traditional Japanese fermented ingredients like sweet rice Koji (Amazake) and a touch of wheat-free white miso to create deep, lingering caramel-like richness.

03

100% Dedicated Gluten-Free & Vegan Workshop

Every donut, waffle, and cup of gelato is handcrafted in our 100% dedicated facility where wheat, dairy, eggs, animal ingredients, honey, and gelatin are strictly prohibited—so guests with celiac disease or severe allergies never have to second-guess a single bite.

FAQ

Visitor Questions

Q01

How many indoor seats do you have? Are strollers, suitcases, and sharing allowed?

We have 12 cozy indoor seats (warm wooden cube stools) where you are welcome to relax and dine in! Every item on our menu is also available for takeout.
• No Minimum Order Rule: There is no one-drink or one-item requirement—sharing a waffle or soft serve with your family or travel companions is completely welcome!
• Strollers & Suitcases Welcome: Our shop is on the 1st floor, and both baby strollers and travel luggage are welcome inside.
• Power Outlet: Available at 1 indoor seat.

Q02

Can I take donuts or ice cream back to my hotel or as souvenirs? Can I pre-order?

Yes! All menu items are available for takeout:
• Boxed Colorful Glazed Cake Donuts (¥430, Soy-Free / contains Almond): Keep at room temperature for about 1 week—making our boxed sets the perfect Fukuoka souvenir or travel gift!
• Plain Baked Soy Donuts (¥330, Nut-Free): Sold flash-frozen to lock in moisture without preservatives. Want to eat one right away? Just ask our staff and we will warm it up for you in-store! At your hotel, microwave at 600W for ~1 minute (or thaw naturally and enjoy within 3 days refrigerated).
• Craft Gelato Cooler Bags: Insulated cooler bags are available for takeout—and you get a FREE cooler bag with ice pack when you purchase 4 or more items!
• Advance Pre-Order / Hold: You can reserve donuts in advance! Sending us a DM on Instagram (@soystories_yakuin) is the most reliable way to hold your items.

Soy Donuts: 600W / 1 minute (or warmed in-store!) • Boxed Cake Donuts: ~1 week at room temp!

Q03

How do I get there by subway? What time is Last Order?

Opening Hours & Last Order: We are open daily from 11:00 AM to 10:00 PM (22:00), with Last Order at 9:45 PM (21:45).
By Subway: Just a 3-minute walk from Yakuin-odori Station (Exit 2) on the Fukuoka City Subway Nanakuma Line—only ~9 minutes directly from Hakata Station and ~4 minutes from Tenjin-minami Station!
By Car: Please use one of the many coin-operated parking lots right next to the shop.

Q04

Is SoyStories a 100% Dedicated Gluten-Free & Vegan Kitchen? What about Nuts, Soy, Honey, or Sesame?

Yes—100% Dedicated Kitchen (Celiac-Safe)! Wheat, dairy, eggs, animal ingredients, honey, and gelatin are never brought into our kitchen or manufacturing facility.
• Tree Nuts (Almond): Our Bubble Waffle batter, Pure Soy Soft Serve, and Plain Baked Soy Donuts (¥330) are 100% Nut-Free. Almonds are used in our Glazed Moist Cake Donuts (¥430), Melting Chocolate Gelato, and the optional Okara Cookie topping.
• Soy-Free Options: All 6 Glazed Moist Cake Donuts (¥430) and 4 fruit Rice Flour Craft Gelato flavors are 100% Soy-Free.
• Sesame: Used only in our Black Sesame Soy Donut and Black Sesame Bubble Waffle.
