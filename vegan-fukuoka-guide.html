Fukuoka Vegan Dessert & Late-Night Café Guide

Stop at SoyStories Yakuin for vegan dessert after dinner in Tenjin or after arriving in Hakata. The café is a 2–3 minute walk from Yakuin-odori Station Exit 2 and is open daily from 11:00 to 22:00 (last order 21:45).

Subway directions How to order Dessert menus

An easy stop on a Fukuoka or Kyushu itinerary

Take the Nanakuma Line to Yakuin-odori Station and walk 2–3 minutes from Exit 2. The subway ride takes about 9 minutes from Hakata or 4 minutes from Tenjin-minami. From Fukuoka Airport, take the Kuko (Airport) Line to Hakata and transfer to the Nanakuma Line. Allow about 20–25 minutes by subway, plus the walk.

Find us on the first floor of Chisan Mansion Dai-3 Hakata, 2-2-24 Yakuin, Chuo-ku, Fukuoka 810-0022.

Hakata connects to Shinkansen and JR Kyushu services, so Yakuin fits into a day trip or an evening in central Fukuoka.

We accept yen cash, major cards, Suica and other Japanese transit IC cards, and PayPay. Suitcases and strollers are welcome.

Order in English and show staff a Japanese card

Tap “Show staff” on an English menu item to open a Japanese counter card. Show the card to staff and select eat-in or takeout.

Browse the English menu and check the 29-allergen reference table before ordering. Our shared kitchen handles soybeans, almonds, cashews, pistachios, macadamia nuts, sesame, Japanese yam, apples, peaches, and oranges. Trace cross-contact is possible.

Takeout includes a free insulated cooler bag and ice pack with four or more gelato cups.

Choose your plant-based dessert

Waffle Soft pairs a freshly baked rice-flour bubble waffle with pure soy milk soft serve (¥690 medium, ¥900 large).

Rice-Flour Craft Gelato comes in eight in-store flavors at ¥490 each. The vegan ice cream guide explains the two styles and lists the four fruit flavors made without soy or nuts.

See the gluten-free sweets guide for waffles, donuts, dorayaki and the dedicated wheat-free kitchen policy, or the SoyStories store guide for the full visit details.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I get late-night vegan dessert in Fukuoka?

SoyStories is open daily from 11:00 to 22:00, with last orders at 21:45. The Yakuin café serves plant-based rice-flour waffles, soy milk soft serve and craft gelato.

How do I get to SoyStories from Hakata, Tenjin or Fukuoka Airport?

Take the Nanakuma Line to Yakuin-odori Station and walk 2–3 minutes from Exit 2. The subway ride takes about 9 minutes from Hakata or 4 minutes from Tenjin-minami. From Fukuoka Airport, take the Kuko (Airport) Line to Hakata and transfer to the Nanakuma Line. Allow about 20–25 minutes by subway, plus the walk.

Can I order without speaking Japanese?

Tap “Show staff” on an English menu item to open a Japanese counter card. Show the card to staff and select eat-in or takeout. The English menu includes a reference table covering Japan’s 29 specified allergens.

Can I take gelato to go and pay with Suica?

Takeout includes a free insulated cooler bag and ice pack with four or more gelato cups. We accept yen cash, major cards, Suica and other Japanese transit IC cards, and PayPay. Suitcases and strollers are welcome.
