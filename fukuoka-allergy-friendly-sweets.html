Dairy-Free, Egg-Free & Wheat-Free Sweets in Fukuoka

SoyStories in Yakuin serves plant-based, gluten-free sweets. If you’re traveling in Fukuoka or Kyushu with a food allergy, check what our kitchen handles and look up allergens by menu item before you order.

Visiting SoyStories Yakuin

Open daily 11:00–22:00. Last order is at 21:45. Strollers and suitcases are welcome.

Take the Nanakuma Line to Yakuin-odori Station. It’s about 9 minutes from Hakata Station or 4 minutes from Tenjin-minami, then a 2–3 minute walk from Exit 2.

From Fukuoka Airport, take the Kuko Line to Hakata and transfer to the Nanakuma Line. Allow about 20–25 minutes by subway.

Find us on the first floor of Chisan Mansion Dai-3 Hakata, 2-2-24 Yakuin, Chuo-ku, Fukuoka 810-0022.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find dairy-free, egg-free and wheat-free sweets in Fukuoka?

SoyStories in Yakuin serves plant-based, gluten-free sweets. Wheat, dairy, eggs, honey, gelatin, and refined white sugar are never brought into our kitchen.

Which gelato flavors are made without soy or nuts?

Berry Mix, Juicy Dragon Fruit, Refreshing Apple, and Too Juicy Peach are made without soy or nuts. Each costs ¥490. Soy and nuts are handled in the shared kitchen, so trace cross-contact is possible.

How can I check allergens before ordering?

Use the interactive 29-allergen menu checker to see the allergens listed for individual items. Read our FoodHug policy and speak with staff before ordering if you have a severe allergy. Trace cross-contact is possible.

Can I visit with a stroller or suitcase, and what are the hours?

Yes, strollers and suitcases are welcome. SoyStories is open daily 11:00–22:00, with last order at 21:45. The shop is a 2–3 minute walk from Exit 2 of Yakuin-odori Station.
