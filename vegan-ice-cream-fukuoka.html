Vegan & Gluten-Free Ice Cream in Fukuoka

SoyStories in Yakuin, Fukuoka serves 100% plant-based (dairy-free and egg-free) and 100% gluten-free (wheat-free) ice cream and gelato. We’re open daily from 11:00 to 22:00, about 4 minutes by subway from Tenjin-minami and 9 minutes from Hakata, plus a short walk.

Two styles of vegan ice cream

Pure Soy Milk Soft Serve

Our soft serve is made with local Kyushu soy milk pressed within two days of use. It comes with five complimentary Japanese toppings: matcha, kinako (roasted soybean powder), hojicha (roasted green tea), vegan chocolate sauce, and kuromitsu (Japanese dark sugar syrup).

Medium ¥460 / Large ¥690

With a freshly baked rice-flour bubble waffle: Medium ¥690 / Large ¥900.

See soft serve menu

Rice-Flour Craft Gelato

8 in-store flavors · ¥490 each

Our vegan gelato in Kyushu is crafted with Japanese rice flour. The flavor lineup features Japanese fermented ingredients like amazake (fermented rice) and white miso, alongside fruit-based recipes.

Four fruit flavors made without soy or nuts: Berry Mix, Juicy Dragon Fruit, Refreshing Apple, and Too Juicy Peach.

Other flavors: Matcha, Hojicha, and Vanilla Coconut contain soy. Melting Chocolate contains soy and almond.

Buy four or more cups to get a complimentary insulated cooler bag and ice pack.

Late-night vegan dessert in Yakuin

Open daily 11:00–22:00. Last order is 21:45.

Take the Nanakuma Line to Yakuin-odori Station, then walk 2–3 minutes from Exit 2. The subway ride takes about 4 minutes from Tenjin-minami or 9 minutes from Hakata. From Fukuoka Airport, take the Kuko Line to Hakata and transfer to the Nanakuma Line. Allow about 20–25 minutes by subway.

Find us on the first floor of Chisan Mansion Dai-3 Hakata, 2-2-24 Yakuin, Chuo-ku, Fukuoka 810-0022. Suitcases and strollers are welcome.

We accept yen cash, major cards, Suica and other Japanese transit IC cards, and PayPay.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I get vegan, dairy-free and gluten-free ice cream in Fukuoka?

SoyStories in Yakuin serves 100% plant-based, dairy-free, egg-free and gluten-free ice cream. Choose from pure soy milk soft serve or eight flavors of rice-flour craft gelato.

Which gelato flavors are made without soy or nuts?

Berry Mix, Juicy Dragon Fruit, Refreshing Apple, and Too Juicy Peach are made without soy or nuts. The shared kitchen handles soy and nuts, so trace cross-contact is possible.

How late can I get vegan dessert at SoyStories?

SoyStories is open daily from 11:00 to 22:00, with last orders at 21:45.

How do I get to SoyStories from Hakata or Tenjin?

Take the Nanakuma Line to Yakuin-odori, about 9 minutes from Hakata or 4 minutes from Tenjin-minami. The shop is a 2–3 minute walk from Exit 2.
